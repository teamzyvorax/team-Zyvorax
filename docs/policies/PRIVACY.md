# Privacy Policy — Draft

**Team ZyvoraX — Privacy Policy**

Status: Product draft. Not legal advice.

## Scope

This draft describes the intended privacy principles for Team ZyvoraX. The final policy must match the deployed product, legal entity, jurisdictions, vendors, and data flows.

## Principles

- Collect only data necessary for the requested service.
- Explain why data is collected before or at collection.
- Protect account and product data with appropriate technical and organizational controls.
- Do not sell personal data.
- Provide deletion/export mechanisms where required by applicable law.
- Keep retention periods documented and limited.

## Current prototype

The current frontend stores demo intent history in browser local storage. It does not require an account and does not send the typed intent to a Team ZyvoraX server.

## Future production requirements

Before production launch, document authentication, database storage, analytics, AI providers, subprocessors, cookies, retention, deletion, international transfers, security controls, legal basis where applicable, and contact details.
